Who converts US dollars to Canadian dollars

Two currencies sharing a name is a genuine source of error, and this is the pair where it shows. Prices on both sides of the border are written with the same dollar sign, so a Canadian price read as an American one looks like a bargain that is not there.

The corridor is dense: cross-border shopping, Canadian snowbirds wintering in Florida and Arizona on Canadian accounts, Canadians employed by American companies, and freight moving constantly in both directions. For all of them this is a daily-life number, not a trading one.

It also moves with oil. The Canadian dollar has a long-standing relationship with crude prices, which is why the pair sometimes shifts on energy news rather than on anything either central bank announced.

Practical notes for USD/CAD

  • Canadian prices exclude GST and provincial sales tax, added at the till and varying by province.
  • Some Canadian retailers accept US dollars at a posted "par" rate that is worse than the market one.
  • Snowbirds spending several months a year in the US often convert in tranches rather than all at once.
  • Currency Converter & Exchange charts the pair up to five years back, which puts an unusual week in perspective.

Both currencies float, so the USD/CAD rate is set by the market rather than fixed — mainly by the interest-rate decisions of Federal Reserve System and Bank of Canada. That is why no static page can print a rate and stay correct.

Questions about USD to CAD

Why do both currencies use the dollar sign?

Both adopted the dollar independently. When a price could be either, look for the CA$ or US$ prefix, or the ISO codes CAD and USD, and convert the figure rather than assuming parity.

Why does the Canadian dollar move when oil prices change?

Energy is a large share of Canadian exports, so demand for crude tends to move demand for the currency alongside it. The effect is a tendency, not a rule, and it can be overridden by interest-rate decisions.

Where does Currency Converter & Exchange get its USD/CAD rate?

From financial data providers, refreshed several times a day whenever the device has a connection. Currency Converter & Exchange timestamps every update, so you can always see how old the figure on screen is.

Does that rate include my bank’s fees?

No. Currency Converter & Exchange shows an interbank reference rate. Banks, card networks and exchange bureaux add their own margin on top, so the CAD you actually receive for your USD will be less.
