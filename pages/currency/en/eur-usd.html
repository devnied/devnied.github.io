Who converts euros to US dollars

Read in this direction, the pair belongs to the European traveller and the freelancer. Someone flying to New York wants to know what their euro budget is worth on the ground; someone invoicing an American client wants to know what a dollar payment will be worth once it lands in a euro account.

Those two needs have different timing. For travel, what matters is the rate on the days you actually spend. For invoicing, it is the rate on the day the payment settles — often weeks after the price was agreed, which quietly turns a fixed-price contract into a currency position nobody intended to take.

It is also the pair most tied to scheduled events. Central bank decisions on both sides are published on a known calendar, and the pair reacts within seconds of each one.

Practical notes for EUR/USD

  • US card terminals may offer to bill you in euros; decline, and pay in dollars.
  • Sales tax is added at the till in most US states, so a shelf price converts to less than you will actually pay.
  • Invoicing in dollars from the eurozone means carrying the currency risk yourself unless the contract says otherwise.
  • Currency Converter & Exchange charts the pair from one day to five years, which shows whether the current level is unusual.

Both currencies float, so the EUR/USD rate is set by the market rather than fixed — mainly by the interest-rate decisions of European Central Bank and Federal Reserve System. That is why no static page can print a rate and stay correct.

Questions about EUR to USD

Should I invoice an American client in euros or dollars?

Invoicing in euros moves the currency risk to the client; invoicing in dollars keeps it with you. If you invoice in dollars, the euro amount you receive depends on the rate at settlement, not at signature.

Why is the converted amount different from the price I see advertised in the United States?

US advertised prices usually exclude sales tax, which varies by state and is added at payment. Convert the final total rather than the shelf price.

Where does Currency Converter & Exchange get its EUR/USD rate?

From financial data providers, refreshed several times a day whenever the device has a connection. Currency Converter & Exchange timestamps every update, so you can always see how old the figure on screen is.

Does that rate include my bank’s fees?

No. Currency Converter & Exchange shows an interbank reference rate. Banks, card networks and exchange bureaux add their own margin on top, so the USD you actually receive for your EUR will be less.

How often does Currency Converter & Exchange update the EUR/USD rate?

Several times a day, from financial data providers, each time the device has a connection. The app shows when the figure was last refreshed.
